# BG3 PS5 controller bindings

Source: bg3.wiki Options page (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), extracted 2026-10-01 by playstation tooling.

## Table 5
- Toggle Tactical Camera: Enter: Hold Exit: [Down with right stick + Up with right stick]
- Camera Backward: [Down with left stick]
- Camera Forward: [Up with left stick]
- Camera Left: [Right with left stick]
- Camera Right: [Left with left stick]
- Camera Rotate Left: [Left with right stick]
- Camera Rotate Right: [Right with right stick]
- Camera Zoom In: [Up with right stick]
- Camera Zoom Out: [Down with right stick]
- Center Camera On Character: [L2]
- Camera Rotate: [Left/Right with right stick]

## Table 6
- Interact: [Cross]
- Highlight Characters: [Right stick press]
- Show Item Labels: [Right stick press]
- Show Sneak Cones: [Right stick press]
- Ping: + + [R2 + L2 + Cross]

## Table 7
- Toggle Group Hide: Hold [Down on directional pad]
- End Turn: [Triangle]
- Cancel End Turn: [Triangle]
