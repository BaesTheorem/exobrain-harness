About

        
Generation is ACE-Step 1.5 (MIT), a foundation model that writes and renders full songs with vocals. Lyrics can come from Claude. Cover art comes from mist-image. Stems come from demucs. UI sounds are CC0 from UI SFX. Layout is Beer CSS on Material Design 3.

        
Free Space quota: each GPU call reserves about three minutes of the daily ZeroGPU pool. When the pool is spent, point the backend at a self-hosted ACE-Step server.
