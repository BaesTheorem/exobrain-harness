## How the room works

1. The expert writes a dossier from the sources.
2. Each writer proposes a form for the whole work, without seeing the other's proposal.
3. The expert writes the plan: the sections in order, who writes each, and what each must do.
4. pen-a writes its sections. Then pen-b writes its sections, and the text is complete.
5. The room revises in cycles. In each cycle all three members critique the latest draft and vote; then each writer revises its own lines. The cycles continue until all three vote that they would sign the text as finished work, as it stands: at least [min] cycles, at most [max].
6. The expert writes a program note. Then an independent reader, who sees only the finished text and the sources, reviews it for the commissioner.

The commissioner does not answer questions during the work: decide everything in the room. It is finished when the three of you say it is.
